Privacy Policy for TapWin

1,000 Levels Speed Reflex Challenge • Arcade Gaming Application
Effective & Last Updated Date: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TapWin ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing an engaging, high-speed 1,000-level arcade reflex challenge. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, synchronize, and protect your information when you download, install, play, or interact with the TapWin mobile application on Android (Application ID: com.aistudio.taprush.kfqm).

By installing or using TapWin, you consent to the data collection, storage, and synchronization practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any terms of this policy, please discontinue use and uninstall the application.

2. Information We Collect

TapWin collects only the information required to operate gameplay levels, synchronize player progress across devices, process voluntary payout/reward requests, and display advertisements:

A. Game Progression & Wallet Data (Room DB & Firebase Firestore Sync)

  • Level Progression: Unlocked levels (Levels 1 through 1,000), completion flags, stars earned (0 to 3 per level), best times, taps-per-second (CPS), and speed bonus records.
  • Daily Missions: Progress on 3 daily challenges, completion dates, and claimed coin rewards.
  • Coin & Currency Exchange Balances: In-game coin balance (coinBalance), converted currency balance (currencyBalanceCents), and exchange transaction history synced to /user_profiles, /exchange_configs, and /exchange_transactions in Firebase Firestore.
  • User Preferences: Sound effects, haptic vibration settings, orb theme unlocks, and ad frequency settings.

B. Payment & Payout Request Information (Voluntary User Submission)

  • Payout Request Details: When you voluntarily submit a request in the "Request Your Payment" section, we collect the information you enter into the form:
    • Account Holder Full Name
    • Phone / Mobile Number
    • Selected Payment Method: Easypaisa, JazzCash, Bank Account, or UPI
    • Account Identifier: Mobile Wallet Number (Easypaisa/JazzCash), Bank Name & Account Number / IBAN, or UPI ID (e.g., name@upi)
    • Optional Notes: Any optional branch, CNIC, or IFSC reference notes you choose to provide, along with the requested coin and currency amount.
  • Purpose & Storage: This data is stored locally in your device's Room database (payment_requests) and synchronized over encrypted HTTPS/TLS to Google Firebase Firestore (/payment_requests) strictly so the administrator can review, verify, and update the status (PENDING, APPROVED, or PAID) of your payout request. We never sell or share your phone number or payout details with third-party marketers.

C. Optional Account Authentication (Google Sign-In)

  • Google Account Credentials: When you sign in with Google via Android Jetpack Credential Manager, we receive your verified Google User ID (UID) and email address via Firebase Authentication to bind your cloud save profile, synchronize your coins, levels, and payment requests across devices, and authenticate administrative access.
  • Play Without Sign-In: You may also play Levels 1–1,000, complete Daily Missions, and use local features without signing into a Google Account.

D. Referral & Invite Information

  • Referral Codes: The app generates a unique referral code (e.g., TR-XXXXXX) for your profile and records redeemed referral codes to award +250 bonus coins to both the new player and the referral owner.
  • Zero Contact Harvesting: TapWin never requests permission to read your contacts or address book. Referral links are shared solely through the native Android system share sheet when you tap Share.

E. Device, Network & Advertising Identifiers

  • Network Connectivity: TapWin checks active network status (Wi-Fi / Mobile Data) via Android connectivity APIs to ensure reliable ad delivery and Firebase cloud synchronization.
  • Advertising ID (AAID): Google AdMob uses the Android Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), IP address, and coarse device diagnostics to serve Banner Ads, Interstitial Ads, and Rewarded Video Ads, and to prevent ad fraud.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To save, synchronize, and restore your 1,000-level progression, coins, and currency ledger across local Room storage and Firebase Firestore.
  • To review, verify, and process player payment requests submitted via Easypaisa, JazzCash, Bank Account, or UPI.
  • To calculate and credit in-game coin rewards for level completions, speed bonuses, rewarded video ads, daily missions, and referrals.
  • To display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video ads via Google AdMob.
  • To prevent clock tampering, duplicate reward claims, and unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

TapWin integrates official Google SDKs for database synchronization, authentication, analytics, and advertising:

Integrated Third-Party Services:
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Serves Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn more at Google Ads Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Analytics): Provides encrypted cloud database storage (/user_profiles, /payment_requests, /exchange_configs, /exchange_transactions, /analytics_telemetry) and Google Sign-In authentication. Learn more at Firebase Privacy & Security.

8. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

We retain your game data and payout request history only as long as necessary to provide the app's services and fulfill your requests.

  • Instant In-App Data Reset: You can immediately wipe all local data (including 1,000-level progress, daily missions, coin balances, exchange transactions, and payment requests) at any time inside the app by going to Settings > Reset Game Data > Reset Everything.
  • Cloud Account & Data Deletion Request: To request permanent deletion of your Google-linked cloud profile, wallet records, and payment request history stored in Firebase Firestore, email business@shaheerrao.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - TapWin". We will permanently erase your cloud records within 7–30 days.

9. Security Safeguards

All communication between the TapWin Android app and Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Access to cloud documents in Firebase Firestore is restricted by strict zero-trust security rules ensuring that each authenticated user can only read or write their own records.
